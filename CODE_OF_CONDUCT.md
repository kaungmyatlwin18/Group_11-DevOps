**Project:** Population Reporting System  
**Module:** SET09803 – DevOps

**Effective date:** To be agreed by the team

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out how Group 11 will work together on the Population Reporting System project. Its purpose is to support respectful communication, fair contributions, accountability, and reliable teamwork throughout the project. All members are expected to follow the agreed Scrum and GitHub workflow.

## 3. Communication and Meetings

- Treat every team member respectfully and professionally.
- Attend agreed sprint planning, reviews, team meetings, and assessed code reviews.
- Notify the team as early as possible if unable to attend or meet a deadline.
- Respond to important project messages within a reasonable time agreed by the team.
- Raise blockers or disagreements early and discuss them constructively.
- Respect personal circumstances and different levels of experience.

## 4. GitHub and Development Rules

- Record and track work through GitHub Issues and the project board.
- Keep issue assignments, sprint fields, and statuses up to date.
- Follow the team's agreed GitFlow branching process.
- Use meaningful commit messages and submit pull requests for review before merging into shared branches.
- Test changes and review pull requests constructively.
- Do not remove, overwrite, or claim another member's work without agreement.
- Document important decisions, bugs, and solutions where appropriate.

## 5. Deadlines and Task Accountability

- Agree on realistic tasks and completion dates during sprint planning.
- Make reasonable efforts to complete assigned work by the agreed deadline.
- Tell the team promptly when a task is blocked or delayed, and work together on a revised plan.
- Discuss unfinished work during sprint reviews and carry it forward transparently if needed.
- Repeated unexplained non-participation may trigger the fair review process in Section 8.

## 6. Contributions and Assessment Evidence

Contributions should be recorded fairly using evidence including:

- GitHub Issues, commits, pull requests, and code reviews.
- Implemented functionality, tests, and documentation.
- Backlog management, sprint planning, coordination, and helping resolve blockers.
- Participation in meetings and assessed code reviews.

The team will agree a single **Individual Contribution Spreadsheet** for the two assessment points. The contribution percentages for **Code Review 1** must total **100%**, and the percentages for **Code Review 2** must separately total **100%**. Percentages must reflect actual contributions; they are not automatically determined by role, commit count, or warnings. Any disagreement may be referred to the module teaching team.

## 7. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following conduct is unacceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, discrimination, insults, or intimidation.
- Taking credit for another member's work or falsifying contribution evidence.
- Deliberately damaging project files or disrupting the team's work.
- Plagiarism, dishonest reporting of completed requirements, or fabricated test results.
- Repeatedly ignoring agreed responsibilities without communication or reasonable explanation.

## 8. Resolving Problems and Corrective Actions

Concerns will be handled proportionately, fairly, and with evidence.

| Situation | Suggested response |
|---|---|
| First minor concern, such as a missed update | Private discussion, support, and an agreed next step. |
| Repeated missed commitments or communication problems | Document the concern and agree on a written improvement plan. |
| Continuing lack of contribution or unresolved disagreement | Review the evidence and contact the module teaching team if necessary. |
| Serious misconduct | Refer the matter promptly to the module teaching team. |

**Procedure:** Identify the concern; gather relevant evidence; give the member an opportunity to explain; consider mitigating circumstances (such as illness, personal difficulties, or technical problems); agree and record corrective steps; and escalate unresolved or serious concerns.

Warnings and internal records do **not** automatically deduct university marks or impose financial fines. The group can discuss contribution percentages, but the teaching team retains authority over formal grading and dismissal decisions. Any proposed dismissal must follow the module's official disciplinary procedure, including the member's opportunity to provide mitigating evidence and appeal.
